Schedule of assignments

You are required to view two films every week. If you cannot locate one of the films, you are permitted to watch another Japanese film from the same year or by the same director. You may find some films on free streaming sites such as Youtube, Vimeo, and Youku. There are also some films available on other pay for streaming services such as Netflix, Hulu, and Amazon, but students will find them to be of less use for this class. You can find many of the films you need for the course at the library in the media room. In addition to the vast library resources holding DVDs at Penn State and Kanopy (a streaming service that PSU subscribes to), students are expected to subscribe to the Criterion Channel, in lieu of a textbook for this class. With Kanopy and the Criterion Channel you can stream many Japanese films to your devices. All films must be watched before the class for which they are listed.

Films are available on DVD, Kanopy through the University Library, available through the Criterion Channel.

All readings are optional except those in boldface.

The schedule is subject to change depending on our progress as a class and on student interests.
