Schedule

Generally. Mondays will involve some lecture or presentations on the mechanics, structure, theories, and history of the short story. Wednesdays will primarily be discussion based session. And Fridays will be experimental: viewing or reading alternative versions of texts we’ve read, creating our own, working collaboratively to develop a theme of discussion from Wednesday, or workshopping our research projects and presentations.

The following course schedule is in flux. Changes will be made depending on student interest, reading rates, and availability of materials. All changes will be posted here at least a week prior to the date of class. Any significant changes will be announced in class and made available for enrolled students on the website.
