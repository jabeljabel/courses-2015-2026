Tentative schedule

This syllabus is in flux. It is a plan that will be modified as our conversations and interests evolve. Consider the list as a directional guide without being a rule. But do complete the readings for week one. As it stands to appeal to the broadest possible set of disparate interests the readings tend toward the philosophical and abstract rather than criticism per se. The work of seminar will be in some measure to fill out the reading list with more practical critical works from our fields.

By the fourth week of class students will be expected to contribute readings for two weeks of the syllabus from week 5 – 15 that relate both to the topic for the week and to their specific fields of interest. The page is editable by registered students. Please edit only after you’ve discussed them with the group in class.

It is recommended that students acquire Jeff Diamanti’s Bloomsbury Companion to Marx as well as Nelson and Grossberg’s Marxism and the Interpretation of Culture for this course as they provide not only several of the readings for the course but great references and sources for further reading. Of course, Terry Eagleton’s books may also prove helpful in this regard.
