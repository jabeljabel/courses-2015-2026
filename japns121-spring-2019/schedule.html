Schedule of assignments

You are required to view two films every week. If you cannot locate one of the films, you are permitted to watch another film from the same year or by the same director. Films are available on DVD or Kanopy through the University Library or available online.

All films must be watched before the class for which they are listed.

All readings are optional except those in boldface.
