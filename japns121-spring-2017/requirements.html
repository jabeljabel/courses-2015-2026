Assignment descriptions and evaluation

Students are required watch two films every week. If you cannot locate one of the films you are permitted to watch another film from the same year or by the same director as those listed in the syllabus. Films are available through the University Library or online. Students may opt to subscribe for the semester to the Filmstruck streaming service, which has most of the films.

If at any point it becomes clear that students have not been viewing the required films, then there will be a pop quiz on the film or films for that week. Since viewing the films is the bare necessity for participation in the class, these quizzes can only work negatively to effect your grade. Each failed pop quiz will deduct 5 points from your class total.

Attendance and participation (10 points)

Students are expected to come to class each week having watched two films on the syllabus.

Students are expected to submit a film response to this dropbox each week responding to the films they have watched.

Film responses must include the following:

  1. Title of the film
  2. Year
  3. Director
  4. Studio
  5. Main Actors
  6. Five Keywords about the film
  7. Three Themes
  8. A Short Analysis of what you think the film means or meant for audiences. Including mention of three significant scenes to back up your analysis
  9. Three Questions about the film.

Film responses should be no longer than one page single spaced.

Responses to films for a given week should be turned in on the Monday night prior to our Tuesday class session for that week. So for instance, week 2 films are due Monday night at midnight on January 16th. That week being an exception insofar as you are allowed to do any and all of the six films (2 screened in class in week one, 2 screened on your own in week one and the 2 screened on your own for week two) for that first response due date.

15 responses are required to get full participation points.

CINEmap geotagging film (10 points)

Research and log location data for 10 scenes from 10 different Japanese films (available on FilmStruck or from the library media center).

  • No more than 3 anime
  • No more than 3 premodern (jidaigeki)
  • At least 4 modern plays (gendaigeki)

Due April 13

Mid term (15 points): time essay

Create a trend

Find three Japanese films from the same year.

Watch them.

Identify a trend.

Write an essay labeling, identifying, and giving the significance for the trend. This will require research on the year beyond what is visible in the film. Get to the library!!

3-5 pages

Due Mar 2, 2017

Final (15 points): space essay

This is the culmination of your work on CINEmap.

Submit a five page research paper on the meaning of the spaces in Japanese films. Drawing on the data you collected, your paper should try to answer ALL of the following questions:

  1. How does film give places meanings?
  2. How do films use the meanings of places?
  3. How did you finding and identify places depicted in the films? What was your process and what were your sources?
  4. How did you research the meanings of those places outside of the films? What was your process for finding information and what were your sources?

Due May 1
