Schedule of readings

This syllabus is in flux. It is a plan that will be modified as our conversations and interests evolve. Consider the reading list as a directional guide without being a rule. But do complete the readings for week one.

As it currently stands, the schedule was designed to appeal to the broadest possible set of disparate interests.

The readings tend toward the philosophical and abstract rather than criticism per se. The work of seminar will be (in some measure) to fill out the reading list with more practical critical works from our fields. As this is done, other works will be removed so as to maintain a reasonable load.

By the third week of class students will be expected to contribute readings for one week of the syllabus from week three – twelve that relate both to the topic for the week and to their specific fields of interest. The page is editable by registered students. Please edit only after you’ve discussed them with the group in class.
