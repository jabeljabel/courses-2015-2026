Course schedule

In order to make this the most rewarding experience, the course schedule will to some extent be in flux for the first month of the course. This will allow us to arrive at some community interests and to determine some of the later readings of the course as a group. Students are encouraged to think about things that are widely available that the want to include in the schedule of readings and suggest them to the class by week four. Because this is the case, please check the canvas version of the syllabus for the most up to date information. Minor changes (such as daily assignments) will be announced in class and changed on the schedule page of canvas. If major changes are made (such as deadline changes), an email announcement will be sent out as well as being announced in class and posted to canvas.

Most course materials will be available as pdf, cbz, or cbr downloads or links to webpages via the course website. Students should become familiar with comic book reader applications that run these file formats. (See Resources) Some links are proprietary and students will have to log into PSU library systems to access. There is a list of suggested books to purchase appended to the course schedule (they can be purchased at any bookseller and most titles are available used). In lieu of a required textbook purchase, students should consider signing up for a one month subscription to Netflix where we will stream some titles unavailable elsewhere.
