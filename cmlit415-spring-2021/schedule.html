Schedule

In order to make this the most rewarding experience, the course schedule will (to some extent) be in flux for the first month of the course. This will allow us to arrive at some community interests and to determine some of the later readings of the course as a group. Because this is the case, please check the canvas version of the syllabus for the most up to date information. Minor changes (such as daily assignments) will be announced in class and changed on the schedule page of canvas. If major changes are made (such as deadline changes), an email announcement will be sent out as well as being announced in class and posted to canvas.

Most course materials will be available as pdf, cbz, or cbr downloads or links to webpages via canvas. Students should become familiar with comic book reader applications that run these file formats. (See Resources) Some links are proprietary and students will have to log into PSU library systems to access. For the class sessions, we will often read parts of books and series; so don’t simply download the file and start reading! Check this schedule for pages or sections. Students should plan on acquiring (through the library or purchasing) the complete version of texts on which they plan to write their research papers.
